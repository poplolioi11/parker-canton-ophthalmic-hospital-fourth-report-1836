# 廣州眼科醫院第四季報（1836） / The Fourth Quarterly Report of the Ophthalmic Hospital at Canton, for the Term Ending on the 4th of November, 1836 — Transcription Dataset

伯駕（Peter Parker）的廣州眼科醫院第四季報記載截至 1836 年 11 月 4 日的診務，收有求診概況、眼科與其他病例、患者敘事和院務帳目。可用於考察十九世紀廣州的教會醫院、患者就醫經驗與臨床記錄方式。

Peter Parker’s fourth quarterly report of the Ophthalmic Hospital at Canton covers the term ending on 4 November 1836. It includes patient narratives, ophthalmic and other cases, hospital activity and accounts. The report documents how an early medical mission hospital described patient care and presented its work to supporters.

## Source and scope

Yale University, Harvey Cushing/John Hay Whitney Medical Library, b21008772, Source frames 1–18. Original imprint: Canton, 1836. All 18 frames are indexed, including covers, blank scans, and the report’s printed pages. Nine tables preserve their printed cells. Two marked omissions occur on printed pages 6 and 8.

## Files

- `transcription.txt` retains line and frame markers. Tabs mark table cells. 
- `transcription.json` identifies each frame and text or table unit. A null printed page means no printed page number was recorded. The source links identify the corresponding scan.
- `transcription.tei.xml` maps the same frame text and table cells into TEI P5.
- `review-notes.md` lists review coverage and reading decisions.
## Transcription and review

AI-assisted reading used fixed source scans. Published text preserves printed spellings and historical terminology. Spacing may be normalized. A square (□) indicates an unresolved character. See the notes for editorial omissions and uncertain readings.

Version 0.2.0. This dataset has no measured error rate or expert proofreading.
